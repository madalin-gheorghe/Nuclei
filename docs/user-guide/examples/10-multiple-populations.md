# Multiple Populations

Run several slime populations together in a shared voxel environment. Each particle constructor has its own movement and sensing settings, so the groups can differ in speed, sensing distance, turning behavior, and signal deposit. Their outputs feed the same solver, where they contribute to and respond to the shared chemoattractant field.

The group colors help you follow their paths within the combined network. Watch how populations with different settings occupy the field and influence the signal encountered by the others. Use this example to explore collective behavior by changing one group's settings while keeping the remaining groups fixed.
