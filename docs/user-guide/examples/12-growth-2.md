# Growth 2

Explore a second balance between slime movement, division, and death. Like Growth 1, this definition uses local neighbor counts to determine which particles can divide or die. Its saved settings allow division at a younger age and within a narrower neighbor-count range, alongside different movement, sensing, and signal settings.

Follow how the population and its trails develop together as these rules are applied. Compare the saved setup with Growth 1, then change one control at a time to identify its effect. This example helps you investigate how local population rules and particle behavior combine to shape growth.
